The gap: capability vs. acceptability

The two questions use different scales (acceptability has 3 levels, capability 5), so simply subtracting rescaled means is hard to interpret. The default view therefore compares proportions of respondents in comparable units and tests the gap within each respondent. The other views are there to check how robust the ordering is to the choice of metric.

Dimensions of the text types

Each of the 25 text types is coded on seven dimensions (our coding; borderline cases are flagged and the codes are editable in the DIM_CODES object in the script). Pick a dimension to see how acceptability, perceived capability and the “sincerity gap” differ across its categories. Values are computed on the currently filtered sample.

Within-respondent paired contrast

For each respondent we average their answers over the text types in category A and in category B, then compare. Shown: mean of A, mean of B, the difference, and the share of respondents with A > B (50% = no difference).

Voice × artistic

Two independent axes: who is speaking lowers acceptability but barely moves perceived capability (→ sincerity gap); artistic character lowers both (→ genuine capability doubt). Cell: accepts at least partial AI · AI capable · concedes capability, rejects AI.
